Finance & back office

Your numbers, made plain

Want a clear read on your numbers? I set up a dashboard that shows your revenue, your margins, and what is slipping. If you choose to share your statements, it updates the moment you drop new ones in.

On top of it, your AI pulls every unpaid invoice and drafts polite follow-ups in your tone, and closes your month in about fifteen minutes instead of an evening. Built with live Excel models, an interactive dashboard, and Claude for Small Business on your own QuickBooks & PayPal.

Inbox & communication

Your inbox stops being a job

If email is eating your day, I set your AI up to read, sort, and draft replies in your own voice, you just review and send.

I also build a written profile of how you write, so everything that goes out sounds like you and not a machine. Built with your Gmail & calendar connected directly, triage and drafting, and the same voice method behind my own writing.

Marketing & getting found

Get found here in town

I run the work a $5,000-a-month agency does to get you found on Google: audits, your map listing, your reviews, and local content, all inside your own account.

Plus a real marketing plan and content in your voice on a schedule, so you're never staring at a blank page. Built with a 12-week local-SEO program, marketing strategy & copy, and content production tailored to Clarksville.

Documents & contracts

Described in a sentence, delivered as the real file

Reports, proposals, contracts, presentations, described in a sentence and delivered as the real file, branded to your business.

Before you sign anything, your AI reads it first and flags the dates, the obligations, and anything easy to miss. Built with real Word, Excel, PowerPoint & PDF production and contract review on your own Docusign. (Not legal advice, always flagged for your attorney.)

Knowledge & systems · flagship

A second brain that runs the place

Everything your business knows, documents, decisions, processes, customer history, in one searchable brain your AI keeps current and that gets sharper every week.

For the businesses ready for it, I build the full operating system, the same kind of system I built for a wealth manager here in town, so the work runs on a schedule instead of waiting on you. Built with a tailored Obsidian + Claude second brain, version history, and a scheduled automation layer.

Getting set up & taught

Set up right, then taught until it sticks

I set up your own account on your own computer, install the tools that match your work, and teach you and your people to use it correctly, on your real work, until it sticks.

I don't set it up and leave. The ongoing side of every engagement is me coming back to teach and keep your system updated as your business changes. Built with your workspace configured with the human-approval model on and your business loaded as permanent context.

Advisory & special projects

Got a one-off? Tell me what you want

If you have a niche need or a special project in mind, I take it on. You tell me what you want done, I research it properly, and I build it for you.

Deep, cited research across dozens of sources; competitor and customer intelligence; and whatever else the project calls for. Built with the full skill library, tailored to your business.
