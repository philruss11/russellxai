Straight answers

Questions I get a lot.

Is the AI going to replace my employees?

No, and I'd be straight with you if I thought otherwise. AI won't take over a business you've run for years. It takes the busywork that eats your week so you and your people can do more of the work that actually matters.

Whose account is all this in?

Yours. Everything I set up lives in your own account, on your own computer, and stays with you. I'm the partner who builds and teaches it, you own it.

What does the $750/month actually pay for?

Me coming back. Ongoing teaching for your team, updates as your business changes, and new services added from the menu over time, without a new price each time.

Do I have to commit to everything at once?

No. We start with the one or two areas costing you the most time, and add more from the menu over time. Your rate is locked at whatever it was when you started, even as it climbs for new clients.

Never. The human-approval model is on from day one. Your AI drafts; you review; nothing sends, posts, or signs without your click.
